The Ribble Estuary in North West England is a tidal estuary that receives fecal coliform loads from its catchment. My MSc thesis, *Hydrodynamic and water quality modeling in the tidal estuaries: The Ribble Estuary case*, modelled the tidal flow in the estuary and used it to predict how those concentrations spread.

**Main contributions**
- Built 3D hydrodynamic models of the tidal forcing in the Ribble Estuary with Delft3D, DIVAST and SWAN, and determined wet–dry areas and the main hydrodynamic quantities
- Tested how different numerical methods, grid generation techniques and turbulence models affect the accuracy and efficiency of the hydrodynamic models
- Wrote my own Navier–Stokes and advection–diffusion solvers in Python and FORTRAN
- Set up D-Water Quality models driven by the hydrodynamic results to predict the distribution of fecal coliform concentrations discharged to the estuary
- Researched sediment transport by using and developing numerical models
