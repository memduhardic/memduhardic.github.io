This project focused on the preparation of **Drought Management Plans for the Seyhan, Ceyhan and Asi Basins**. The work involved supporting basin-scale drought planning studies by assessing hydrological and meteorological drought conditions, evaluating water availability and sectoral water use, and contributing to the development of mitigation measures and action plans.

**Main contributions**
- Drought analysis for current and future periods
- Water availability and sectoral water use assessment
- Drought risk and vulnerability evaluation
- Development of drought mitigation measures and action plans

**Client**
- Directorate General of Water Management (DGWM), Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry

**Type**
- Drought Management Planning
- Hydrological Analysis
