This EU and Government of Türkiye-funded project provides **technical assistance for the preparation of River Basin Management Plans for six basins in Türkiye**. My work focused on the integrated modeling framework that supports basin planning, covering hydrology, water allocation, and water quality, as well as the integration of climate change and drought analyses into the planning process.

The project aims to support evidence-based, sustainable water resources management by providing consistent modeling tools and scenario analyses for basin planning and sectoral water allocation.

**Main contributions**
- Developed and calibrated the basin-wide modeling framework, including river network topology, hydrological simulation, water allocation, and water quality modeling using WEAP and AquaTool (EVALHID–SIMGES–GESCAL)
- Integrated climate change impacts into modeling workflows through evaluation of multiple Global Climate Models, statistical and dynamical downscaling, bias-corrected climate forcings, and trend and scenario analyses
- Led drought characterization and risk assessment, including hydrologic drought indices, spatiotemporal analysis, and the interaction between low-flow conditions and water quality deterioration
- Supported inter-model coupling and data harmonization across hydrological, water allocation, and water quality modules
- Used remote sensing and Earth observation datasets (Sentinel-1/2, ERA5, MODIS) for watershed characterization, model inputs, and machine learning-based classification and prediction tasks
- Contributed to the preliminary design of real-time decision support systems (RT-DSS), including monitoring network assessment, proposed monitoring points with cost estimates, and a SCADA-based framework linking real-time observations with hydrological and water allocation models
- Delivered technical presentations to clients, regulatory bodies, and stakeholders, and took part in field investigations for data collection and validation of model assumptions
